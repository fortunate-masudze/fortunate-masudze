# Fortunate Masudze

### eLearning Content Developer | Moodle Specialist | Instructional Design

I am an eLearning Support and Instructional Design professional with a Bachelor of Science in Information Technology and practical experience in developing, converting and managing digital learning content in higher education.

I specialise in transforming traditional academic materials into engaging, structured and self-paced online learning experiences using Learning Management Systems and eLearning authoring tools.

My experience includes Moodle course management, interactive content development, SCORM integration, digital learning support and instructional design.
